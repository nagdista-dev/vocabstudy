## What it is

VocabStudy is a free, open-source web app for Arabic speakers learning
English vocabulary. The learner adds a word they actually met, and the app
provides the Arabic meaning, an English definition, a real example and
pronunciation. The learner writes a sentence from their own life, then
recalls the word on spaced days.

## What makes it different

- **Arabic-first explanations.** The meaning is in Arabic, and the app
  explains mistakes Arabic speakers make with the word: `depend on` (not
  `depend of`), `borrow` vs `lend`, `actually` ≠ "currently", `advice`
  uncountable, and common collocations.
- **Active recall.** The app asks the learner to produce the word from the
  meaning or from a sentence with a gap. It does not ask "do you know it?".
- **Local-first privacy.** There is no server that stores words and no account.
  Words and progress stay on the device.
- **Offline.** The app is a PWA and works without a connection after the
  first visit.
- **Free and open source.** The code is MIT-licensed and public.

## Limits (stated honestly)

- No ready-made word lists or graded course from A1 to C1.
- No automatic sync between devices.
- No native iOS or Android app.
- The interface is English, not Arabic.
- It focuses on vocabulary, not a complete conversation or grammar course.

## How it compares

VocabStudy is not a replacement for Anki, Memrise, Quizlet, Duolingo or
LanGeek. It is an Arabic-first word notebook for the words a learner meets.
Anki is more powerful and customizable; Memrise and LanGeek offer ready-made
courses; Quizlet is built around class sets; Duolingo is built around a daily
habit. The full comparison, with a disclosure that the author makes
VocabStudy, is at:

- Arabic: https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/ar/best-vocabulary-apps/
- English: https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/en/best-vocabulary-apps/
