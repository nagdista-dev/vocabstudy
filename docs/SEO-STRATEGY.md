## 1. The job

Make VocabStudy the first app an Arabic speaker finds when they search for a
way to remember English words — in Google, in Bing, and in AI answers
(ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot).

Two things are true at the same time:

1. The product is genuinely different: Arabic-first meanings and usage traps,
   active recall, spaced days, offline, no account, free.
2. Nobody can find it. On 2026-11-28, `site:vocabstudy.nagdista.com` returned
   **zero results** on Google, Bing and Brave. The domain is new (repo created
   2026-11-15), has no backlinks and no content pages.

So this is not a "fix the meta tags" job. It is: build an indexable content
layer, make the product legible to machines, then earn the first mentions.

### What success looks like

| Horizon | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 2–4 weeks | Pages indexed in Google + Bing | 10 of 10 |
| 1–3 months | Search impressions for Arabic queries | first impressions, then growth |
| 3–6 months | Clicks from non-brand queries | first 100/month |
| 3–6 months | AI answers that mention or cite VocabStudy | at least 1 of the tracked prompts |
| Ongoing | Branded searches ("VocabStudy") | month-over-month growth |

Rankings are not promised. Indexing, technical correctness, content quality and
measurement are.

## 3. Positioning

**Arabic:** VocabStudy تطبيق مجاني للعرب لحفظ كلمات الإنجليزية: تضيف الكلمة اللي
قابلتها، وتكتب جملة عنها، وتراجعها في أيام متباعدة لحد ما تثبت. بدون حساب،
بدون إعلانات، ويعمل بدون إنترنت.

**English:** The free, open-source vocabulary app built for Arabic speakers:
add the English words you actually meet, learn the mistakes Arabic speakers
make with them, and recall them on spaced days until they stay.

**Always qualify the name.** `vocabstudy.com` and an App Store app already use
"VocabStudy". Every title, OG tag and directory listing says *for Arabic
speakers* or *للعرب*.

**Honest limits** (state them, never hide them):
- no ready-made word lists — the library starts empty by design;
- no native iOS/Android app — it is an installable web app (PWA);
- no automatic sync — device-to-device transfer is manual (QR/file);
- interface in English; Arabic appears in meanings, traps and explanations;
- a new, small, open-source project.

## 5. Content rules

1. **Arabic pages in clear MSA, warm not academic.** The app and `about.html`
   stay Egyptian; marketing pages target all 22 Arab countries and match how
   people search. English terms stay in Latin script inside `<span lang="en">`.
2. **Every claim is checkable.** Review intervals (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60), 112
   usage traps, 30,000-word frequency list, MIT licence, no account. No user
   counts, no ratings, no testimonials, no "#1".
3. **Disclose the bias.** The comparison pages say plainly: "we make
   VocabStudy". Trust is the ranking strategy.
4. **Give the answer first.** Each page opens with a direct answer in 2–3
   sentences, then the detail. This is what AI engines extract.
5. **Tables and lists over walls of text.** One idea per paragraph, short
   sentences, descriptive headings that match real questions.
6. **Date everything.** Visible "last updated" plus `dateModified` in schema.
7. **Link out.** Official sites for every competitor; sources for research
   claims. Outbound links are part of being a good source.
8. **No AI tells.** No "ليس X بل Y", no forced triads, no "في عالم اليوم",
   no em-dash chains. Read it aloud test.

## 7. GEO / AI visibility

AI engines cite sources that are crawlable, specific, structured and
corroborated. The plan:

1. **Be readable.** Static HTML, no JS required for content, clean headings,
   direct answers, tables.
2. **Be specific.** Named numbers, dates, limits, prices ("free"), platforms.
3. **Be corroborated.** GitHub repo, `nagdista.com`, MCP directory listings,
   AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, Hacker News, Arabic communities. AI models
   trust facts that appear in more than one place.
4. **Be machine-readable.** `llms.txt` for a curated map, `facts.md` for a
   citable fact sheet, `pricing.md` for the free/open-source answer.
5. **Track prompts manually.** Five Arabic and five English prompts, three
   runs each, monthly. Recorded in `marketing/ai-visibility-baseline.md`.

Baseline on 2026-11-28:
- Google AI Overview (AR: أفضل تطبيق لحفظ الكلمات الإنجليزية) → Memrise,
  2Shine, British Council, Quora. VocabStudy absent.
- ChatGPT (EN: best vocabulary app for Arabic speakers) → Anki, Memrise,
  Quizlet, Drops, Duolingo; notes most apps are not built for Arabic speakers.
  VocabStudy absent.

## 9. 90-day roadmap

**Weeks 1–2 — foundation**
- Ship technical SEO, 8 pages, schema, `llms.txt`.
- Verify GSC + Bing, submit sitemap, run IndexNow.
- Update GitHub README and repo description (after approval).

**Weeks 3–6 — distribution**
- Product Hunt launch (prepare assets, choose a Tuesday–Thursday).
- AlternativeTo, Slant, OpenAlternative, awesome lists, MCP directories.
- Show HN with an honest "I built this" post.
- Arabic communities: answer questions, link only when it fits.

**Weeks 7–12 — content and links**
- Two new guides based on GSC queries that already get impressions.
- Outreach to 10 Arabic English-teaching blogs/teachers with a free, honest
  resource (not a guest-post blast).
- Cross-link from `nagdista.com` and `hassala.nagdista.com` (needs approval).
- Re-run the AI prompt log; publish a "state of Arabic vocabulary apps" piece
  if the data supports it.

## 10. Risks and what we do about them

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Brand collision with `vocabstudy.com` | always qualify "for Arabic speakers"; target long-tail first; build branded search |
| New domain, no authority | start with low-competition Arabic long-tail; earn mentions; no paid links |
| Thin/duplicate pages | each page has a distinct job; canonical + hreflang; no spun content |
| AI answers are non-deterministic | log 3–5 runs per prompt; track trends, not a single answer |
| Competitors change features/prices | comparison pages date-stamped; link to official pages; review quarterly |
| Content drifts from the product | `tools/seo_check.py` + build; update pages when features change |

---

## 11. Deliberately not doing

- Fake reviews, ratings or user counts.
- Paid links or link exchanges.
- Cloaking, hidden keyword blocks, doorway pages.
- Mass DMs or spam in Facebook/WhatsApp groups.
- An analytics or ad script that breaks the privacy promise.
- Translating the app UI (the English UI is a deliberate learning feature).
