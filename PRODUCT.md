# Product

## What it is
VocabStudy is a free, open-source, local-first app for **Arabic speakers
learning English vocabulary**. The learner tells it a word they want to
remember; the app fills in the rest, teaches it, and brings it back on spaced
days (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60) until it stays. It ships with no words. No account,
no server, no ads, offline — everything on the learner's device.

## Who it is for
An Arabic-speaking adult (A2–C1) — a student, an employee, someone preparing
for IELTS or a job abroad — who studies on a phone in short bursts and on a
laptop in long sessions (up to six hours a day). Many think in Egyptian or
another dialect, and are tired of apps that feel translated or built for
someone else.

## The promise
"Your words, remembered for good — taught the way a good Arab teacher would."
- The app and its interface are in English: every screen is practice. Arabic
  appears where it teaches: the meaning of each word.
- It knows the mistakes Arabs make: P/B, V/F, silent letters, false friends
  (actually ≠ حالياً), an Arabic keyboard left on.
- It never feels like a machine: short human sentences, one clear next step.

## Surfaces and mode
The whole app is an **Operate** surface: a study tool. Precision, calm and a
clear next step outrank expression. Identity lives in details: the yellow
highlighter on every word being learned, ink-blue actions, a hyperlegible
face for English words and a well-sized Arabic face for meanings.

## Principles
1. One next step on every screen.
2. Remember, not just recognise: active recall first.
3. The learner's data never leaves the device.
4. English interface; Arabic only where it teaches.
5. Open source and readable: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript anyone can learn from.

## Surfaces
One home: **the library**. Add a word at the top, today's plan under it, then
every word with filters. Activities (learn, recall, quiz, spell, match, use it,
speak, Quick 5) open from there and come back to it. Words also arrive from
outside: Share → VocabStudy, the bookmarks-bar button, From text.

## What makes it a teacher, not a word list
1. **Use it**: the learner writes their own sentence and the app marks it.
2. **Usage traps**: the mistakes Arab learners make with each word, and why.
3. **Where you met it**: the learner's own sentence is the first recall cue.
4. **Stress you can see**, and real recorded voices.
And a week that ends with a report (Saturday → Friday, ready Thursday).

## Out of scope (for now)
Built-in word lists, guessed CEFR levels, grammar lessons, cloud sync (devices
meet by QR code instead), accounts, anything paid. (Dark mode was out of scope
until v10 V6; it now ships as a Night theme — Light / Night / Auto.)
