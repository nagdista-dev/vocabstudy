VocabStudy or Anki? Which one fits you?

Both are free on most platforms, and both use spaced repetition. The difference is setup time, Arabic support, and how much control you want. Here is the honest comparison.

Last updated: 28 November 20267 min readBy Nagdista

The short answer

If you want the strongest spaced-repetition system, full customization, and you do not mind setup time, choose Anki. If you want a simple Arabic-first app that keeps the words you meet and explains Arabic-speaker traps, with no account, choose VocabStudy. They are not identical alternatives: Anki is a powerful general tool, and VocabStudy is an Arabic-first word notebook.

The difference in two lines

Anki is a general flashcard program that gives you full control over every card and every algorithm. It is the first choice for people who want to build their own system, or who are preparing for a big exam and have time to set it up.

VocabStudy is an Arabic-first web app that starts empty, brings the Arabic meaning, an example and pronunciation, and explains the mistakes Arabic speakers make. It is for people who want to start now, not build a system.

Anki: power and cost

What Anki does well

  • Spaced repetition: the modern FSRS algorithm adjusts each card’s schedule based on how hard it is for you, not a fixed table. That saves review time over the long run.
  • Control: you can design the card however you want: image, audio, sentence, gap, definition, translation. You can change everything later.
  • Library: thousands of shared decks, including IELTS, TOEFL, medical and legal vocabulary.
  • Sync: a free AnkiWeb account moves your cards between desktop and phone.

What you may not like

  • Setup: the first hour can go into choosing a template, arranging fields and understanding the settings. Many people stop there.
  • Arabic content: shared decks are mostly English. You will need to write the Arabic meaning yourself or find a suitable Arabic deck.
  • No trap explanations: Anki shows what you put in it. It will not tell you that depend needs on, or that advice has no plural.
  • A feeling of complexity: add-ons and templates can become a project of their own, instead of a means to learn.
When Anki is the right choice

If you want one system that serves you for years, you are preparing for a specific exam, and you are willing to set up the cards once and then keep going. Also if you like full control and do not mind a less simple interface.

VocabStudy: simplicity and limits

What VocabStudy does well

  • A fast start: you add the word, and it brings the meaning, definition, example and pronunciation. No templates, no settings.
  • Arabic first: the meaning is in Arabic, and 112 usage traps are written for an Arabic-speaking learner.
  • Active recall: it asks you to type the word from the meaning or from a sentence with a gap, not just “do you know it?”.
  • Privacy: no account, and no server that stores your words. Your words live in your browser, and you can export a complete backup.
  • Offline: it works as an installed app after the first visit.

What you may not like

  • No ready-made lists: the library starts empty. If you want a course from A1 to C1, this is not it.
  • Fixed review: the intervals 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 do not adapt to each word’s difficulty the way FSRS does.
  • No automatic sync: moving between devices is manual. Fine if you use one device, less convenient if you switch between phone and laptop.
  • English interface: Arabic is in the meanings and explanations, but the buttons and sentences are English.
  • A new project: it does not have Anki’s community or add-on library.
When VocabStudy is the right choice

If your problem is that you meet many words and never save them, and you want a solution that starts in minutes, explains Arabic-speaker errors, and asks for no email or subscription.

Which should you choose?

Choose Anki if you…

Are preparing for IELTS, TOEFL or a university exam, want ready decks and full control, and have an hour to set up the system. Or if you already use Anki and want to keep going.

Choose VocabStudy if you…

Want to keep the words you meet in reading and work, need Arabic meanings and trap explanations, and prefer a simple app with no account. Or if you tried Anki and stopped at the setup.

Should you use both?

It is possible, but not with the same words. Make VocabStudy your daily notebook for new words, and use Anki for one specific exam deck. If you find yourself moving words between them, you are spending review time on moving.

Questions

Is Anki really free?

Anki is free on desktop and Android. The iPhone app (AnkiMobile) is a one-time purchase that supports development. Sync through AnkiWeb is free. Check Anki’s official page for current prices.

Is VocabStudy a complete replacement for Anki?

No. VocabStudy is simpler and more Arabic-first, but less flexible, with fixed review intervals and no deck marketplace or add-ons. It is a replacement for people who want simplicity, not for people who want maximum control.

Which is better for a beginner?

If the beginner meets new words and needs Arabic explanations, VocabStudy is the easier start. If they are preparing for a specific exam and need a ready curriculum, Anki with a suitable deck may be better, provided someone helps with the setup.

Can I move my words from Anki to VocabStudy?

Usually yes. Export your cards from Anki as a clean CSV file, then import it into VocabStudy. Check the columns (word, meaning, example) before importing. See the import page in the app.

Are VocabStudy’s fixed intervals less effective than FSRS?

FSRS is more precise, especially with thousands of cards. The fixed 1–60 day intervals are enough for most learners, particularly with active recall and real use. The difference grows with very large libraries and many years of study.
