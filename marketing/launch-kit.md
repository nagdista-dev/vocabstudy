# VocabStudy launch kit

**Goal:** put VocabStudy in front of Arabic speakers who are already looking
for a way to remember English words, without spam, fake reviews or paid links.

**Canonical link:** https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/
**Arabic hub:** https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/ar/
**Comparison page:** https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/ar/best-vocabulary-apps/
**How-to guide:** https://vocabstudy.nagdista.com/ar/how-to-memorize-english-words/

**Rule:** every community post discloses that you built the app. Trust is the
only distribution advantage a new, free product has.

---

## 1. The pitch

### Arabic

**One line:** دفتر كلماتك الإنجليزي، بالعربي.

**Short:** VocabStudy تطبيق مجاني للعرب لحفظ كلمات الإنجليزية التي تقابلها
فعلًا. تضيف الكلمة، تكتب جملة عنها، وتراجعها في أيام متباعدة حتى تثبت. بدون
حساب، بدون إعلانات، ويعمل بدون إنترنت.

**Long:** أغلب تطبيقات المفردات تبدأ بقائمة جاهزة. VocabStudy يبدأ فارغًا.
تضيف الكلمة التي قابلتها في مسلسل أو مقال أو إيميل، فيجلب معناها بالعربي
وتعريفها ومثالًا عليها ونطقها، ثم تكتب جملة من حياتك. بعد ذلك يعيدها عليك في
اختبارات قصيرة بعد يوم، ثم 3 أيام، ثم 7، ثم 14، ثم 30، ثم 60. مع شرح عربي
لـ112 خطأ شائعًا عند العرب. مجاني ومفتوح المصدر، ويعمل بدون إنترنت.

### English

**One line:** The English words you meet, made to stay.

**Short:** VocabStudy is a free, open-source vocabulary app for Arabic
speakers. Add the words you actually meet, learn the traps Arabic speakers
make, and recall them on spaced days. No account, no ads, offline.

**Long:** Most vocabulary apps start with a ready-made list. VocabStudy starts
empty. You add a word you met in a series, an article or a work email, and it
brings the Arabic meaning, an English definition, a real example and
pronunciation. You write a sentence from your own life. Then it brings the
word back in short recall tests after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days, with 112
hand-written usage traps for Arabic speakers. Free, open source, and offline.

### The honest limits (say them before someone else does)

- No ready-made word lists or graded course. The library starts empty.
- The interface is English. Arabic is in the meanings and explanations.
- No automatic sync between devices. Transfer is manual (QR or file).
- It is an installable web app, not a native App Store or Google Play app.
- It is a new, small, open-source project.

Do not start with "everyone who learns English". Start with one audience and
one channel. The first 100 users will tell you which one is real.

## 3. Rules of the launch

1. **Disclose.** "أنا بنيت التطبيق" / "I built it" in every post.
2. **Read the rules.** Many groups ban links or self-promotion. Ask a mod
   before posting. If the answer is no, thank them and move on.
3. **Help first.** Answer three questions in a community before you share a
   link. A post that only drops a link gets removed, and deservedly so.
4. **One post per community.** Do not cross-post the same text to five
   subreddits in an hour.
5. **No fake accounts, no upvote requests, no review swaps.** Product Hunt and
   Reddit detect this, and it destroys the only thing a free app has: trust.
6. **No mass DMs.** Personalized outreach only, one follow-up, then stop.
7. **No "best app" claims.** Say what it does and who it is for.
8. **Take criticism well.** If someone says the interface should be Arabic,
   explain the learning reason; if they find a factual error, fix it.
9. **Do not promise rankings or user numbers.** You do not have them yet.
10. **Measure what you can.** GSC, Bing, GitHub, comments. Do not invent
    traffic numbers.

Do not do everything in week one. A launch that lasts four weeks beats a burst

## 5. Channel playbooks

### 5.1 Product Hunt

Product Hunt is an English-speaking maker audience. It will not bring many
Arabic learners, but it brings a backlink, a public launch page and a reason
for other directories to list the app.

**Listing**

- **Name:** VocabStudy
- **Tagline (59 chars):** Keep the English words you meet. Free, offline, no account.
- **Topics:** Education, Productivity, Open Source, Web App, Android
- **Description (under 260 chars):** Free, open-source vocabulary app for
  Arabic speakers. Add the words you actually meet, get Arabic meanings and
  112 usage traps, then recall them on spaced days. Offline, no account, no ads.
- **Gallery:** logo, three screenshots (home, word card, recall), one GIF of
  adding a word, one 45–60 second video.
- **First comment:** see section 6.4.

**Launch day**

1. Publish at 12:01 AM Pacific Time; the day resets then.
2. Post the link on X and LinkedIn, not with "please upvote" but with "we are
   live on Product Hunt today; feedback welcome".
3. Reply to every comment within two hours.
4. Do not offer discounts, gifts or anything else for upvotes.
5. At the end of the day, write down the three most useful comments. They are
   better than the ranking.

### 5.2 Hacker News (Show HN)

HN rewards technical honesty and punishes marketing language. Lead with the
constraint: static PWA, no backend, localStorage, service worker, MIT.

- **Title:** `Show HN: Free offline vocabulary app for Arabic speakers (no account, MIT)`
- **Body:** see section 6.5.
- **When:** a weekday morning, US Eastern time. Avoid Friday afternoon and
  weekends.
- **After posting:** stay in the thread. Answer questions about storage,
  offline behaviour, the dictionary sources and the limits. Do not ask for
  upvotes. Do not argue about the name collision with `vocabstudy.com`; explain
  that this one is qualified as "for Arabic speakers".

### 5.3 Reddit

Start with one subreddit where the post is genuinely useful. Read the rules
first; some ban self-promotion entirely.

Candidate communities:

- `r/languagelearning` — broad, allows thoughtful "I built this" posts with
  disclosure.
- `r/EnglishLearning` — learners and teachers; value-first tips work better
  than a link drop.
- `r/Anki` — comparison and "why not Anki" questions; be honest that Anki is
  more powerful.
- `r/arabic` — language and culture; only if the post is about Arabic
  speakers learning English, not a generic ad.
- `r/InternetIsBeautiful` — possible if the app is free and unusual; read the
  rules, no self-promo unless allowed.

Post format that works:

1. A specific problem ("Arabic speakers don't usually need another 5,000-word
   list; they need the usage traps").
2. What you built and why.
3. One concrete example (`depend on`, `borrow/lend`, `actually`).
4. Honest limits.
5. Link at the end, with disclosure.
6. Ask for feedback on one thing, not for upvotes.

### 5.6 X / Twitter

- Pin a five-tweet thread in Arabic (section 6.1).
- Post one tip per day for a week, each with a screenshot or a one-line demo.
- Quote or reply only when the conversation is genuinely about vocabulary,
  Arabic speakers or Anki. Do not hijack unrelated threads.
- Use at most two hashtags: `#تعلم_الإنجليزية` and `#EnglishLearning`. More
  looks like spam.
- Do not tag influencers and ask for a share.

### 5.7 LinkedIn

Two posts:

1. Arabic, for teachers, trainers and L&D people (section 6.2).
2. English, for the open-source and privacy angle.

LinkedIn rewards a story and a specific detail, not a feature list. Mention
the 112 traps and the "no account" decision; those are the memorable parts.

### 5.8 TikTok, Reels and Shorts

Five scripts, 25–40 seconds each, 9:16, Arabic voiceover, burned-in captions.
No face required: screen recording plus a clean voiceover is enough.

1. **"3 كلمات إنجليزي بتستخدمها غلط"**
   Hook: "لو بتقول depend of، الفيديو ده ليك."
   Show: depend on, borrow/lend, actually.
   CTA: "التطبيق فيه 112 خطأ زي دول، مجاني وبدون حساب."

2. **"ليه الكلمة بتضيع منك؟"**
   Hook: "مش because you forgot. لأنك شفتها مرة واحدة."
   Show: recognition vs recall, a gap-fill question.
   CTA: "جرّب تستدعي الكلمة قبل ما تشوف الإجابة."

3. **"100 كلمة في يوم"**
   Hook: "جربت تحفظ 100 كلمة في يوم؟ هنساهم كلهم."
   Show: why 5–10 a day with review beats a cram.
   CTA: "الخطة الأسبوعية في الدليل المجاني."

4. **"تطبيق ببلاش وبدون حساب"**
   Hook: "عايز دفتر كلمات على موبايلك؟"
   Show: add a word, Arabic meaning, example, pronunciation, install to home
   screen, airplane mode.
   CTA: "الرابط في البايو."

5. **"5 دقايق مراجعة"**
   Hook: "عندك 5 دقايق؟ راجع 10 كلمات."
   Show: the 5-minute review, a recall question, the result.
   CTA: "مش محتاج حساب."

Keep the screen recording clean: no personal words, no notifications, no
browser tabs with private data.

## 8. Launch-day checklist

- [ ] Deploy is green: app check + SEO check.
- [ ] The ten URLs return 200.
- [ ] `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt` and the IndexNow key file are live.
- [ ] OG image preview looks right on X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp.
- [ ] Test on a real phone: add a word, write a sentence, go offline, reload,
      install to home screen.
- [ ] Prepare three screenshots, one GIF and one 45–60 second video.
- [ ] Publish the X thread and pin it.
- [ ] Publish the LinkedIn post.
- [ ] Publish Product Hunt (if launch day) and the first comment.
- [ ] Publish Show HN (a different day; do not stack every channel).
- [ ] Post in one community where you have permission.
- [ ] Reply to comments for at least two hours.
- [ ] Ask five new users "how did you find us?" and write down the answers.
- [ ] End of day: note what worked, what did not, and one thing to change.

## 9. Metrics

| Signal | Where | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Indexed pages | GSC → Pages | the technical foundation is working |
| Impressions | GSC → Performance | Google understands the pages |
| Clicks | GSC → Performance | the titles and descriptions attract the right people |
| Bing impressions/clicks | Bing Webmaster Tools | early signal, often faster than Google |
| GitHub stars/forks | GitHub | developer and open-source interest |
| Product Hunt comments | Product Hunt | qualitative feedback, not a goal |
| AI mentions/citations | `marketing/ai-visibility-baseline.md` | whether AI answers can find and trust the pages |
| "How did you find us?" | feedback replies | the only attribution that works without analytics |

Do not report a number you cannot reproduce. Do not compare a launch-day spike
with a monthly baseline.

## 10. What not to do

- No fake reviews, ratings, user counts or testimonials.
- No paid links, link exchanges or mass guest posts.
- No mass DMs or unsolicited group posts.
- No asking for upvotes on Product Hunt, Reddit or HN.
- No arguing with mods or critics.
- No "best app" or "learn English in 30 days" claims.
- No ads before the landing pages have real conversion data.
- No translating the app UI to chase a keyword.
- No analytics script that breaks the privacy promise.

## 11. After the launch

1. Read the comments; group them into product issues, copy issues and
   positioning issues.
2. Fix the one product issue that blocks the most people.
